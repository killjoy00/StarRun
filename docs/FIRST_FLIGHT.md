# First flight

New pilots start in the Debris Belt with the Vanguard and its usual Aegis.
First Flight uses the regular controls, weapons, scout packs and attack wings.

| Combat time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | A short centered popup explains autopilot as the usual Level 1 scouts fly in. |
| 0:15 | A centered prompt pauses just before the first sniper fires. The player can use Aegis or keep flying. |
| 0:23 | The first draft recommends Arc coil. The next attack wing depends on the weapon picked. |
| 0:30 | Another sniper charges. The regular beam warning gives players a chance to time Aegis on their own. |
| 0:48 | The second draft recommends upgrading the weapon again and says what the rank adds. |
| By 1:08 | The Harvester arrives shortly after the last wave clears, or at 1:08 while any remaining escorts are still flying. Its main gun destroys the Vanguard and the pilot ejects. |
| Debrief | Pilot recovery pays 15 Cores once. The player can buy their first rank of Hull plating for 7 Cores and launch a regular campaign with a new ship. |

Every new ship flies in from offscreen. Lower incoming damage lets players reach
the scripted ending even if they skip Aegis. Both sniper beams can be blocked
with Aegis. The Harvester's main gun ends the flight and the pilot recovery pays
the one-time reward. The ending grants no campaign win, achievements or unlocks.
Players can skip from the title or pause menu. The Guide offers a replay
without another recovery payout when the run slot is free. Existing suspended
first flights keep their stage timing and conclusion.
