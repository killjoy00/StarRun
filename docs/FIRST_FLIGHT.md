# First flight

Start first flight on a new profile starts an authored Debris Belt sortie with the
Vanguard and its usual Aegis. Keep the ordinary playfield, enemies, weapons,
damage, ability cooldowns and upgrade cards. Use the existing art and audio.

| Beat | Play | Presentation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch | A small scout wave enters immediately. | Short engine flare and an autopilot caption. |
| First beam | A sniper charges a real beam. If Aegis is ready, freeze just before impact so the player can use the actual ability button or keep flying. A second sniper gives an early cast time to recharge. | Readable beam lane, a caption and a marker over Aegis. |
| Draft one | Pick Arc coil, Prism lance or Seeker pods. The next waves bring clustered ships and beam chargers. | The usual draft cards, followed by a short installation cue. |
| Draft two | Improve the chosen weapon, add the other offered weapon, or take Coolant loop. | The usual draft cards and an optional speed hint after the first pick. |
| Harvester | A shorter Harvester encounter with stronger fire below half health. | The regular boss entrance and a brief clear animation. |
| Debrief | A win earns 15 Cores once. Hull plating costs 7 Cores for the first rank. Launch a regular seeded campaign directly. A loss offers a retry or campaign launch. | Ship art, the actual result and earned Cores. |

The intro ends in the Belt. Campaign achievements, Codex, unlocks and Daily
records come from regular play. Players can skip from the title or pause menu.
The guide offers a replay when no other live run occupies the save slot.

## Automated playthrough

At 1×, combat markers were 0.78 seconds to the first kill, 15.50 seconds to
the guided Aegis cast, and upgrades at 0:35 and 1:22. The first chosen
weapon dealt damage by 0:37. The Harvester arrived at 2:25. All nine draft
combinations cleared in 2:38–2:57 of combat with well-timed defensive play,
including an early cast and a missed lesson. Reading and boss presentation
pause combat, so elapsed play time can be longer. The fixture also verifies
a real loss, replay, durable payout, and a second flight launched with actual
Hangar plating.

The screenshot matrix covers small phones and a 1280×800 desktop/Deck-sized
viewport. First-time player feedback and native device feel require playtesting.
