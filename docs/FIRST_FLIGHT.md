# First flight

New pilots start in the Debris Belt with the Vanguard and its usual Aegis.
The sortie uses the regular controls, weapons, upgrade cards and enemy attacks.

| Combat time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Scouts arrive as the Vanguard launches. The first caption explains autopilot. |
| 0:15 | The first sniper beam gives the player a paused Aegis prompt. A later sniper gives an early cast time to recharge. |
| 0:23 | The first draft recommends Arc coil. The next group is arranged for Arc coil, Prism lance or Seeker pods, depending on the pick. |
| 0:48 | The second draft recommends upgrading that weapon again and says what the rank adds. The next formation gives the player a chance to see the difference. |
| 1:08 | The Harvester arrives and charges its main gun. The gun destroys the Vanguard and an escape pod gets the pilot out. |
| Debrief | Pilot recovery pays 15 Cores once. The player can buy their first rank of Hull plating for 7 Cores and launch a regular campaign with a new ship. |

The main gun is a scripted ending. The earlier sniper beams work normally and
can be blocked with Aegis. The ending records the ship as lost and pays the
one-time recovery reward. It grants no campaign win, achievements or unlocks.
Players can skip from the title or pause menu. The Guide offers a replay
without another recovery payout when the run slot is free. Existing suspended
first flights keep their original encounter schedule and conclusion.

## Automated playthrough

The first kill arrives in under one second. The first guided cast is at 0:15.50,
and the Harvester destroys the ship around 1:11 of combat. Drafts and the boss
entrance pause the timer. All nine upgrade combinations reach the ejection with
or without Aegis, and the recommended second Arc coil adds a visible chain
target in the following formation.

The test suite checks old and new checkpoints, Aegis timing, reward recovery
without a duplicate payment, and the first campaign launch. The screenshot
matrix includes the ejection with full and reduced effects. Native device feel
and first-time player reactions need playtesting.
