# Star/Run Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 16, 2026**

This policy explains how **Star/Run**, the space auto-shooter game, handles information when you play the game and when you use optional services such as Apple Game Center or advertising.

## Game progress stored on your device

Star/Run keeps game progress, purchased in-game upgrades, settings, achievements, saved flights, and Daily Challenge results on your device. Those save files are used to resume the game and remember your progress. You do not need to create a Star/Run account to play.

The game developer does not operate a separate account server to receive those local save files. You can remove locally stored progress by deleting the app and its data using your device's settings. Depending on your device's backup settings, some app data may also be included in backups controlled by your device provider.

## Optional Apple Game Center

On supported iPhone versions, Star/Run can connect to **Apple Game Center** to authenticate an optional player profile, submit Daily Challenge leaderboard scores, and report achievements. This information is processed by Apple under its own privacy terms and Game Center settings.

You can manage Game Center and your Apple account's privacy settings through your device's Settings app. Star/Run continues to support offline play if Game Center is unavailable or you choose not to use it.

## Advertising in versions that include ads

Some Star/Run versions may use **Google AdMob** to show banner ads on menu and results screens. Not every version contains or enables ads. When ads are enabled, the **Google Mobile Ads SDK** and Google's consent-management tools may process information, including:

- Device identifiers, such as advertising or app-related identifiers, where available and permitted;
- IP address and approximate location derived from it;
- Ad impressions, ad interactions, and app usage information;
- Diagnostic, crash, and performance information.

Google and its advertising partners may use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve ad services, subject to applicable consent requirements and your device's privacy settings. Which information is collected can depend on your app version, region, consent choices, and SDK configuration.

Where required, the app presents consent or privacy choices before requesting ads. If an **Ad privacy choices** option is available in the app's Settings screen, you can use it to review or change your choices. Your device may also provide privacy controls for advertising identifiers and app tracking.

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from apps or sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Information shared with third parties

Star/Run does not use its own server to publish your local save files. When you choose Apple Game Center or use an ad-enabled version, the relevant provider may process information under its own policies. We do not control the independent collection or processing performed by Apple or Google.

Star/Run does not ask you to enter a name, postal address, or email address to play. If you contact support, you may choose to provide information to help resolve your issue; do not post private or sensitive information in a public issue.

## Children's privacy

Star/Run is not designed to require a child's personal information to play. Advertising settings and any required age-related treatment must match the audience and age-rating classifications chosen for the version distributed. If you believe personal information has been handled improperly, use the contact link below.

## Changes

The information handled by Star/Run can change as optional features, platform services, or advertising integrations change. Updates to this policy will be published at this same URL with a revised date.

## Questions or privacy requests

For questions about Star/Run's handling of data or requests related to the game, contact the developer through the [Star/Run support page](https://github.com/killjoy00/StarRun/issues). This opens a public GitHub issue and requires a GitHub account. **Do not post personal information in a public issue.** If your request concerns data held by Apple Game Center or Google AdMob, their respective privacy controls and support channels may also be relevant.
